The primal is a maximization LP with constraints \(\sum_i w_i x_i\le W\) and \(x_i\le 1\) for each \(i\), and \(x_i\ge 0\). We assign a dual variable to each primal constraint: \(z\ge 0\) for the capacity constraint and \(y_i\ge 0\) for each constraint \(x_i\le 1\). The dual minimizes over the constraint right-hand sides, and for each primal variable \(x_i\) it has one constraint collecting the coefficients of \(x_i\) (namely \(w_i\) in the capacity row and \(1\) in the \(i\)-th row), which must be at least the objective coefficient \(b_i\): \[\min\ Wz+\sum_i y_i \quad\text{s.t.}\quad w_i z + y_i \ge b_i\ \ \forall i,\qquad y_i\ge 0\ \ \forall i,\qquad z\ge 0.\]

Intuition. One price \(z\) per unit of capacity plus a price \(y_i\) per item, set just high enough that every item's value is covered.
