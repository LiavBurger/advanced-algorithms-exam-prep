We show that positive semidefiniteness of \(A\) (meaning \(v^TAv\ge 0\) for all \(v\)) forces every eigenvalue of \(A\) to be non-negative.

  1. Take an eigenpair. Let \(\lambda\) be any eigenvalue and \(u\neq 0\) a corresponding eigenvector, so \(Au=\lambda u\).
  2. Evaluate the quadratic form at \(u\). By the PSD assumption \(u^TAu\ge 0\). On the other hand, substituting \(Au=\lambda u\), \[0\le u^TAu=u^T(\lambda u)=\lambda\,u^Tu=\lambda\|u\|_2^2.\]
  3. Conclude. Since \(u\neq 0\) we have \(\|u\|_2^2>0\). Dividing the inequality \(\lambda\|u\|_2^2\ge 0\) by the positive number \(\|u\|_2^2\) yields \(\lambda\ge 0\). As \(\lambda\) was an arbitrary eigenvalue, all eigenvalues of \(A\) are non-negative.
