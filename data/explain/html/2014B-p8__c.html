Goal Write DLP1, the dual of the vertex-cover LP relaxation LP1.

Strategy Apply the mechanical dual rules: one dual variable per primal constraint, one dual constraint per primal variable.

Key idea Each edge constraint becomes a variable \(y_{uv}\ge0\); each vertex \(v\) becomes the constraint that its incident edge-weights sum to at most \(1\).

Why this move Dualizing is a fixed recipe once the primal is in standard form, so we turn the crank rather than invent anything.

  1. Dual variables. One \(y_{uv}\ge0\) per edge constraint \(x_u+x_v\ge1\).
  2. Dual objective. The primal right-hand sides are all \(1\), so the dual maximizes \(\sum_{uv\in E} y_{uv}\).
  3. Dual constraints. Each variable \(x_v\) (objective coefficient \(1\)) appears in the constraints of edges touching \(v\), giving \(\sum_{u\in N(v)} y_{uv}\le1\) for every \(v\).

So DLP1 is \(\max\sum_{uv\in E} y_{uv}\) s.t. \(\sum_{u\in N(v)} y_{uv}\le1\) for all \(v\) and \(y_{uv}\ge0\): put weights on edges so each vertex's total is at most \(1\), a fractional matching.
