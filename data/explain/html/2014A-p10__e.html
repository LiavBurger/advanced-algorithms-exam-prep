Goal compute the objective value at the vertex reached after the first pivot.

Strategy plug the new variable values into the non-basic form of the objective from part (c).

Key idea that form already bakes in the constant, so evaluating it is just substitution.

Why this move the non-basic form is the cleanest place to read the objective, since the leftover non-basic variables are all \(0\).

  1. Take the objective. From part (c) it is \(20+x_2+2x_3-2s_1\).
  2. New values after the pivot. Basis \(\{x_1,x_3\}\), so \(x_2=s_1=0\) and \(x_3=6\).
  3. Substitute. \(20+0+2(6)-0=32\).

So the objective is \(32\) — which matches the intuition: \(x_3\) climbed \(6\) units at rate \(+2\), adding \(12\) to the starting \(20\).
