The primal from part (a) has \(|V|\) equality constraints, so the dual has \(|V|\) variables, a free vector \(y\in\mathbb{R}^{|V|}\). Applying the standard dual rule to \(\min -\mathbf{1}^\top x\) s.t. \([\,A_G\mid I\,]\begin{bmatrix}x\\ s\end{bmatrix}=\mathbf{1},\ x,s\ge 0\), the dual is

\[\max\ \mathbf{1}^\top y\quad\text{s.t.}\quad [\,A_G\mid I\,]^\top y\le\begin{bmatrix}-\mathbf{1}\\ \mathbf{0}\end{bmatrix}.\]

Splitting the constraint by column-block, the \(x\)-columns give \(A_G^\top y\le -\mathbf{1}\) and the slack columns give \(I y\le\mathbf{0}\), i.e. \(y\le 0\). To simplify, change variables \(y\mapsto -y\): the objective becomes \(\min\mathbf{1}^\top y\), the constraint \(A_G^\top y\le-\mathbf{1}\) becomes \(A_G^\top y\ge\mathbf{1}\), and \(y\le 0\) becomes \(y\ge 0\). The clean dual is

\[\min\ \mathbf{1}^\top y\quad\text{s.t.}\quad A_G^\top y\ge\mathbf{1},\ \ y\ge 0,\]

which per edge \((u,v)\) reads \(y_u+y_v\ge 1\).

Intuition. The slack columns are just the identity, so their dual constraints simply pin the sign of \(y\), and one sign flip cleans everything up.
