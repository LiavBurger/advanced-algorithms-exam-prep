Goal write the dual of the LP in (b).

Strategy apply the mechanical duality rules to the primal \(\max\sum_v x_v\) s.t. \(x_u+x_v\le 1,\ x\ge 0\).

Key idea one dual variable \(y_{uv}\) per edge; the dual minimizes, primal right-hand sides become the dual objective, and primal objective coefficients become the dual right-hand sides.

Why this move the primal is in the clean "max, \(\le\) constraints, \(x\ge 0\)" form, so its dual is "min, \(\ge\) constraints, \(y\ge 0\)" read straight off the rules.

  1. Dual variables. One \(y_{uv}\ge 0\) per primal constraint, i.e. per edge \((u,v)\in E\).
  2. Dual objective. Each edge constraint has right-hand side \(1\), so we get \(\min\sum_{uv\in E}y_{uv}\).
  3. Dual constraints. One per primal variable \(x_v\). Variable \(x_v\) appears in exactly the edge constraints touching \(v\), each with coefficient \(1\), and its objective coefficient is \(1\); this gives \(\sum_{u:\,(u,v)\in E}y_{uv}\ge 1\) for every \(v\).
  4. The dual. \(\min\sum_{uv\in E}y_{uv}\) s.t. \(\sum_{uv\ni v}y_{uv}\ge 1\) for all \(v\in V\), and \(y\ge 0\) — the total \(y\) on edges touching each vertex is at least \(1\).
