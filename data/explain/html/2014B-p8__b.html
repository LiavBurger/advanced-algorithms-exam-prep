Goal Write LP1, the relaxation of the vertex-cover ILP IP1.

Strategy Drop integrality: replace \(x_v\in\{0,1\}\) by \(x_v\ge0\), and note the upper bound \(x_v\le1\) is unnecessary.

Key idea A relaxation enlarges the feasible region from the box \(\{0,1\}\) to the interval \([0,1]\), and for this min-problem the cap \(x_v\le1\) never binds.

Why this move Relaxing an ILP always means loosening the integrality constraint into the tightest continuous version, so we just swap \(\{0,1\}\) for \(\ge0\).

  1. The relaxed program. \[ \min \sum_{v\in V} x_v \quad\text{s.t.}\quad x_u+x_v\ge1\ \ \forall uv\in E,\qquad x_v\ge0\ \ \forall v\in V. \]
  2. Why no \(x_v\le1\). The objective minimizes and all constraints on \(x_v\) are lower bounds. If some optimum had \(x_v>1\), lowering it to \(1\) still satisfies every edge (an endpoint of \(1\) already makes \(x_u+x_v\ge1\)) and decreases the objective.

So no optimal solution ever needs a coordinate above \(1\); that's why \(x_v\le1\) is redundant.
