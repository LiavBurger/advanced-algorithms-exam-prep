Take the dual from part (b) and impose integrality, \(y_D\in\{0,1\}\), to get the binary integer program \[ \max\sum_{D\in C}y_D\quad\text{s.t.}\quad\sum_{D:\,i\in D}y_D\le1\ \ \forall i\in S,\qquad y_D\in\{0,1\}. \] We read off what this asks combinatorially.

  1. Since \(y_D\in\{0,1\}\), the variable \(y_D\) is the indicator of "subset \(D\) is chosen". Let \(\mathcal{D}=\{D:y_D=1\}\) be the chosen collection.
  2. The objective \(\sum_D y_D=|\mathcal{D}|\) is the number of chosen subsets, which we maximize.
  3. The constraint \(\sum_{D:i\in D}y_D\le1\) says that each element \(i\in S\) belongs to at most one chosen subset. Equivalently, no two chosen subsets share an element — the chosen subsets are pairwise disjoint.
The BIP is the set packing problem: find a maximum-size collection of pairwise disjoint (mutually non-overlapping) subsets from \(C\).
