Let \(X\) be the random variable counting the number of steps a random walk started at \(s\) takes to first reach \(t\). Since a walk that has visited all vertices has in particular visited \(t\), the time to reach \(t\) is at most the cover time, so by the class bound of \(2mn\) on the cover time we have \(\mathbb{E}[X]\le 2mn\).

Because \(X\ge 0\), Markov's inequality gives \[\Pr[X>8mn]\le \frac{\mathbb{E}[X]}{8mn}\le \frac{2mn}{8mn}=\frac14.\] Therefore the walk reaches \(t\) within \(8mn\) steps with probability at least \[1-\Pr[X>8mn]\ge 1-\frac14=\frac34.\]

Intuition. The walk expects to hit \(t\) within \(2mn\) steps, so giving it four times that budget makes overshooting unlikely (at most \(1/4\)) by Markov.
