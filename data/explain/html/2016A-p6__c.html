The LP in (b) is a (fractional) weighted vertex cover. Read each variable \(y_v\ge 0\) as an amount of weight (resource) placed on vertex \(v\). The constraint \(y_u+y_v\ge w(uv)\) says that for every edge \(uv\), the weight sitting on its two endpoints must total at least its weight \(w(uv)\). The objective \(\min\sum_v y_v\) asks to do this using as little total weight as possible.

In words: put enough weight on the vertices so that for each edge \(e\) the amount on its two endpoints is at least \(w(e)\), while minimizing the total weight used. It is fractional because we are in the LP relaxation rather than the integer program.

Intuition. Cover every edge's weight using the cheapest total load spread over its endpoints.
