The variable \(x_3\) enters the basis first.

We rewrite the objective using only the non-basic variables \(x_2,x_3,s_1\). Substituting \(x_1=10-2x_2-s_1\),

\[ 2x_1+5x_2+2x_3=2(10-2x_2-s_1)+5x_2+2x_3=20+x_2+2x_3-2s_1 . \]

Raising a non-basic variable from \(0\) changes the objective at the rate of its coefficient: \(+1\) for \(x_2\), \(+2\) for \(x_3\), and \(-2\) for \(s_1\). The largest positive rate is \(+2\), belonging to \(x_3\), so \(x_3\) is chosen to enter the basis.

Intuition. Write the profit in terms of the variables currently at zero and push the one that pays the most per unit.
