We must show \((D)\) is the LP dual of the set-cover relaxation. The relaxation replaces \(x_i\in\{0,1\}\) by \(x_i\ge0\): \(\min\sum_i c_i x_i\) s.t. \(\sum_{i:S_i\ni j}x_i\ge1\) for every element \(j\), \(x\ge0\). The clean way is to write it in matrix standard (equality) form and use the known dual rule.

  1. Matrix form. Let \(A\) be the \(n\times m\) incidence matrix with \(A_{j,i}=1\) if \(j\in S_i\) and \(0\) otherwise. Then constraint \(j\) is \((Ax)_j\ge1\), i.e. \(Ax\ge\mathbf 1\). With cost vector \(c=(c_1,\dots,c_m)\) the relaxation is \(\min c^\top x\) s.t. \(Ax\ge\mathbf 1,\ x\ge0\).
  2. To equality standard form. Rewrite \(Ax\ge\mathbf1\) as \(-Ax\le-\mathbf1\) and add nonnegative slacks \(s\ge0\): \(-Ax+s=-\mathbf1\). Stacking variables, this is \([-A\mid I]\begin{bmatrix}x\\s\end{bmatrix}=-\mathbf1\) with \(x,s\ge0\), and the objective is still \(\min c^\top x\) (slacks have zero cost).
  3. Apply the standard-form dual rule. For \(\min \hat c^\top z\) s.t. \(Mz=\hat b,\ z\ge0\), the dual is \(\max \hat b^\top y\) s.t. \(M^\top y\le\hat c\), with \(y\) free. Here \(M=[-A\mid I]\), \(\hat b=-\mathbf1\), \(\hat c=(c;\,0)\). So the dual is \(\max -\mathbf1^\top y\) s.t. \(\begin{bmatrix}-A^\top\\ I\end{bmatrix}y\le\begin{bmatrix}c\\0\end{bmatrix}\), i.e. \(-A^\top y\le c\) and \(y\le0\).
  4. Substitute \(y\mapsto -y\) (so the new \(y\ge0\)). Then \(-\mathbf1^\top y\) becomes \(\mathbf1^\top y=\sum_{j=1}^n y_j\), and \(-A^\top y\le c\) becomes \(A^\top y\le c\), i.e. \(\sum_{j:j\in S_i}y_j\le c_i\) for each \(i\). This is exactly \((D)\).
Dual \((D)\): \(\displaystyle\max\sum_{j=1}^n y_j\ \ \text{s.t.}\ \ \sum_{j:\,j\in S_i}y_j\le c_i\ \ \forall i,\qquad y_j\ge0.\)
