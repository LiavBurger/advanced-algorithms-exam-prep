Goal Write the dual of the maximization LP from part (a).

Strategy Apply the standard recipe: for \(\max\;c^\top x\) with \(Ax\le b,\ x\ge0\), the dual is \(\min\;b^\top y\) with \(A^\top y\ge c,\ y\ge0\).

Key idea Each primal constraint becomes a dual variable, and each primal variable becomes a dual constraint.

Why this move The problem is already in canonical \(\max,\le,\ge0\) form, so the dual is just the mechanical transpose of that recipe.

  1. Dual variables and objective. Three constraints give \(y_1,y_2,y_3\ge0\); their right-hand sides \(12,7,10\) form the objective \(\min\;12y_1+7y_2+10y_3\).
  2. One dual constraint per primal variable. Read each column of \(A\) and set it \(\ge\) that variable's objective coefficient: \[3y_1+y_2+2y_3\ge2,\quad y_1-3y_2+y_3\ge4,\] \[y_1+2y_2+3y_3\ge3,\quad 4y_1+3y_2-y_3\ge1.\]
  3. Assemble. The dual is \(\min\;12y_1+7y_2+10y_3\) subject to those four "\(\ge\)" constraints and \(y_1,y_2,y_3\ge0\).
