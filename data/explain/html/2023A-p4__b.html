The primal from part (a) is \(\min -\mathbf{1}^\top x\) s.t. \(A_G x+Is=\mathbf{1},\ x,s\ge 0\). We dualize it and then simplify.

  1. One dual variable per equality. There are \(|V|\) equality constraints (one per vertex), and equality constraints produce free (unconstrained) dual variables, so the dual has a vector \(y\in\mathbb{R}^{|V|}\).
  2. Apply the standard dual rule. For \(\min c^\top w\) s.t. \(Mw=\mathbf{1},\ w\ge 0\) the dual is \(\max \mathbf{1}^\top y\) s.t. \(M^\top y\le c\). Here the variable is \(w=(x,s)\), the constraint matrix is \(M=[\,A_G\mid I\,]\), and the cost is \(c=(-\mathbf{1},\,\mathbf{0})\) (\(-1\) on each \(x\)-coordinate, \(0\) on each slack). The block \(M^\top y\le c\) splits into two families: \[A_G^\top y\le -\mathbf{1}\quad(\text{from the }x\text{ columns}),\qquad I^\top y\le \mathbf{0}\ \Rightarrow\ y\le 0\quad(\text{from the slack columns}).\] So the dual is \(\max \mathbf{1}^\top y\) s.t. \(A_G^\top y\le -\mathbf{1},\ y\le 0\).
  3. Clean up by flipping signs. Substitute \(y\mapsto -y\). Then \(\mathbf{1}^\top y\) becomes \(-\mathbf{1}^\top y\) (so \(\max\) turns into \(\min \mathbf{1}^\top y\)), \(A_G^\top y\le-\mathbf{1}\) becomes \(A_G^\top y\ge\mathbf{1}\), and \(y\le 0\) becomes \(y\ge 0\).
\[ \min\; \mathbf{1}^\top y\qquad\text{s.t.}\qquad A_G^\top y\ge \mathbf{1},\quad y\ge 0. \]

Reading \(A_G^\top y\ge\mathbf{1}\) row by row: for each edge \((u,v)\), \(y_u+y_v\ge 1\).
