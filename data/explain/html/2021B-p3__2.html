We prove the greedy algorithm achieves a \(\tfrac23\)-approximation: the legally colored weight is at least \(\tfrac23\) of the optimum. Let \(v_1,\dots,v_{|V|}\) be the vertices in the order they are processed, and let \(A_i,B_i,C_i\) denote the three classes at the moment \(v_i\) is considered (so they contain only vertices processed before \(v_i\)).

  1. Per-vertex bound. When \(v_i\) is placed, it is added to the class minimizing \(w(v_i,\cdot)\). The minimum of three numbers is at most their average, so the illegal weight created at this step is \[\min\big(w(v_i,A_i),w(v_i,B_i),w(v_i,C_i)\big)\le \tfrac13\big(w(v_i,A_i)+w(v_i,B_i)+w(v_i,C_i)\big).\]
