Goal write the semi-matching program for the drawn graph in matrix/vector form and give its dual BIP.

Strategy make each vertex a constraint row and each edge a column; the entry is \(1\) when the edge touches the vertex, the right-hand side is the vertex's degree cap, then transpose for the dual.

Key idea the constraint matrix is just the vertex-edge incidence matrix, so building it is bookkeeping: mark which edges hit which vertex.

Why this move once the abstract program from part (1) is in hand, plugging in a specific graph is only filling the incidence matrix, and the dual is its transpose by the standard recipe.

  1. Read the graph. \(V_1=\{a,b,c\}\), \(V_2=\{d,e\}\), edges \(e_1=ad,\ e_2=ae,\ e_3=bd,\ e_4=be,\ e_5=ce\).
  2. Fill the rows. One row per vertex: \(a\!:(1,1,0,0,0)\), \(b\!:(0,0,1,1,0)\), \(c\!:(0,0,0,0,1)\), \(d\!:(1,0,1,0,0)\), \(e\!:(0,1,0,1,1)\), collected into matrix \(A\).
  3. Primal. Caps \(1\) for \(a,b,c\) and \(2\) for \(d,e\): \(\max\,(1,1,1,1,1)\,x\) s.t. \(Ax\le(1,1,1,2,2)^{\mathsf T}\), \(x_i\in\{0,1\}\).
  4. Dual. Swap: cost vector becomes the primal right-hand side, matrix transposes, direction flips: \(\min\,(1,1,1,2,2)\,y\) s.t. \(A^{\mathsf T}y\ge(1,1,1,1,1)^{\mathsf T}\), \(y_j\in\{0,1\}\).

Each dual constraint is one edge asking that at least one of its two endpoints be chosen — the vertex-cover flavor made concrete for this graph.
