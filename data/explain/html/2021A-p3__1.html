\(G\) is a complete graph whose every edge costs \(1\) or \(2\), and we want a \(\tfrac43\)-approximation for TSP. The first step is given: since \(G\) is complete it has a cycle cover, so let \(C\) be a minimum-cost cycle cover — a set of vertex-disjoint cycles that together visit every vertex exactly once, of least total edge cost (computable in polynomial time). We must finish the algorithm by turning \(C\) into a single tour.
