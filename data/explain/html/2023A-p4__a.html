We relax the integer program by dropping the integrality constraint, and then put the resulting LP into standard form (a minimization with equality constraints and nonnegative variables). Two changes are needed. First, turn the maximization into a minimization by negating the objective: \(\max\sum_{(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\) becomes \(\min\big(-\sum_{(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\big)\). Second, turn each inequality into an equality by adding a nonnegative slack variable \(s_v\) for every vertex \(v\). This gives

\[\min\ -\sum_{(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\quad\text{s.t.}\quad \sum_{u:(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}+s_v=1\ \text{ for all }v\in V,\qquad x_{u,v}\ge 0,\ s_v\ge 0.\]

Here \(s_v=1-\sum_{u}x_{u,v}\) just measures how far vertex \(v\) is from being fully matched. More succinctly, let \(A_G\) be the \(|V|\times|E|\) incidence matrix of \(G\) (column \((u,v)\) has a \(1\) in rows \(u\) and \(v\) and \(0\) elsewhere). Then the LP is

\[\min\ -\mathbf{1}^\top x\quad\text{s.t.}\quad A_G x+I s=\mathbf{1},\qquad x,s\ge 0,\]

where \(x\) has length \(|E|\) and \(s\) has length \(|V|\).

Intuition. Negate the objective and let one slack per vertex soak up the unused capacity, and the matching LP is already in the shape simplex wants.
