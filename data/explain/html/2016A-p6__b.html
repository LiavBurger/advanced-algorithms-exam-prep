The LP relaxation from (a) is \(\max\sum_{uv\in E}w(uv)\,x_{uv}\) subject to \(\sum_{uv\ni v}x_{uv}\le 1\) for every vertex \(v\), and \(x_{uv}\ge 0\). It is a maximization with "\(\le\)" constraints, so its dual is a minimization with "\(\ge\)" constraints: one dual variable \(y_v\ge 0\) per vertex constraint, and one dual constraint per primal variable \(x_{uv}\), that is, per edge.

The edge \(uv\) appears only in the two vertex constraints of \(u\) and \(v\), each with coefficient \(1\), and its objective coefficient is \(w(uv)\), so its dual constraint is \(y_u+y_v\ge w(uv)\). The primal right-hand sides are all \(1\), so the dual objective is \(\min\sum_{v}y_v\). The dual is

\[\min\ \sum_{v\in V}y_v \quad\text{s.t.}\quad y_u+y_v\ge w(uv)\ \ \forall uv\in E,\qquad y_v\ge 0\ \ \forall v\in V.\]

Intuition. Each vertex constraint of the primal turns into a dual variable, and each edge turns into a dual constraint.
