Every bin other than the last one opened holds items of total weight at least \(1-\tfrac1k\).

The reason a new bin was opened for the item of weight \(1/k\) is that this item did not fit into any existing bin. That means every existing bin had less than \(1/k\) of free space at that moment, so each of them was already filled to more than \(1-\tfrac1k\). Since this was the last time a new bin was opened, all later items are only added to existing bins, and no bin's load ever decreases. Therefore, in the final packing, every bin except the last-opened one contains items of total weight at least \(1-\tfrac1k\).

Intuition. The algorithm opens a new bin only as a last resort, so the moment it does, every other bin must have been almost completely full.
