We start from the matching integer program \(\max\sum_{(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\) subject to \(\sum_{u:(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\le 1\) for every vertex \(v\), \(x\ge 0\), and integrality. Dropping the integrality constraint gives an LP; putting it in standard form means: a minimization objective, equality constraints, and all variables nonnegative — the form the simplex algorithm needs.

  1. Maximize to minimize. \(\max\sum x_{u,v}\) becomes \(\min\big(-\sum_{(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\big)\); negating the objective flips the sense without changing the optimizer.
  2. Inequalities to equalities. Each vertex constraint \(\sum_{u:(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\le 1\) has slack, so add one nonnegative slack variable \(s_v\ge 0\) per vertex: \(\sum_{u:(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}+s_v=1\). Recovering the original: \(s_v=1-\sum_u x_{u,v}\ge 0\) is exactly the "\(\le 1\)" condition, and \(s_v\) measures how far vertex \(v\) is from being saturated.
\[ \min\; -\sum_{(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}\quad\text{s.t.}\quad \sum_{u:(u,v)\in E}x_{u,v}+s_v=1\ \ \forall v\in V,\qquad x_{u,v}\ge 0,\ s_v\ge 0. \]

Compactly, let \(A_G\) be the \(|V|\times|E|\) incidence matrix — its column for edge \((u,v)\) has a \(1\) in rows \(u\) and \(v\) and \(0\) elsewhere. Writing \(x\in\mathbb{R}^{|E|}\) and \(s\in\mathbb{R}^{|V|}\), the program is \(\min -\mathbf{1}^\top x\) s.t. \(A_G x+Is=\mathbf{1},\ x,s\ge 0\), which is standard form.
