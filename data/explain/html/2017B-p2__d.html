We form the LP dual of the relaxation from part (c). Mechanically, each primal constraint becomes a dual variable, and each primal variable becomes a dual constraint; a max-primal becomes a min-dual.

  1. List the primal constraints and assign dual variables. The primal (in \(x\)) is \(\max\sum_i b_i x_i\) subject to the single capacity row \(\sum_i w_i x_i\le W\) and the \(n\) upper bounds \(x_i\le 1\). Give the capacity row a dual variable \(z\ge 0\) and each upper bound \(x_i\le 1\) a dual variable \(y_i\ge 0\). (Both are \(\ge 0\) because these are \(\le\) constraints in a maximization.)
  2. Dual objective = combination of the primal right-hand sides. The capacity RHS is \(W\) and each upper-bound RHS is \(1\), so we minimize \(Wz + \sum_i y_i\).
  3. One dual constraint per primal variable \(x_i\). Collect the coefficients of \(x_i\) across the primal constraints: it appears with coefficient \(w_i\) in the capacity row (multiplied by \(z\)) and coefficient \(1\) in its own bound (multiplied by \(y_i\)). Their sum must be at least the objective coefficient \(b_i\): \[w_i z + y_i \ge b_i \quad \forall i.\]
  4. Sign constraints. Since the primal variables \(x_i\ge 0\), the dual constraints are inequalities \(\ge\) (not equalities), and the dual variables satisfy \(y_i\ge 0,\ z\ge 0\).

The dual is \[\min\ Wz + \sum_i y_i \quad\text{s.t.}\quad w_i z + y_i \ge b_i\ \forall i,\qquad y_i\ge 0\ \forall i,\qquad z\ge 0.\]
