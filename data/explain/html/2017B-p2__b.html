For each item \(i\) introduce a variable \(x_i\in\{0,1\}\), where \(x_i=1\) means item \(i\) is chosen and \(x_i=0\) means it is left out. We want maximum total cost subject to total weight at most \(W\): \[\max \sum_i b_i x_i \quad\text{s.t.}\quad \sum_i w_i x_i \le W,\qquad x_i\in\{0,1\}\ \ \forall i.\] The objective sums the cost of the chosen items, the constraint keeps their total weight within the capacity, and the integrality \(x_i\in\{0,1\}\) forces each item to be either fully in or fully out.

Intuition. One 0/1 switch per item: pack it or not, maximize cost without busting the weight budget.
