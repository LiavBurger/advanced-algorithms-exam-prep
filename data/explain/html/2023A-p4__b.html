Goal Write the dual of the standard-form matching LP from part (a), simplified as much as possible.

Strategy Apply the standard dual rule, split the constraint by the \(x\)-block and the slack-block, then flip signs to clean it up.

Key idea The slack columns are the identity, so their dual constraints simply bound the dual variables' sign.

Why this move Every equality-constrained primal has a mechanical dual, and the only work left is reading the two column-blocks and tidying the signs.

  1. One variable per equality. The primal has \(|V|\) equality constraints, so the dual has a free vector \(y\in\mathbb{R}^{|V|}\), and the dual is \(\max \mathbf{1}^\top y\) s.t. \([\,A_G\mid I\,]^\top y\le(-\mathbf{1},\mathbf{0})\).
  2. Split the blocks. The \(x\)-columns give \(A_G^\top y\le -\mathbf{1}\); the slack columns give \(I y\le \mathbf{0}\), i.e. \(y\le 0\).
  3. Flip signs to simplify. Substitute \(y\mapsto -y\): the objective becomes \(\min \mathbf{1}^\top y\), the constraint becomes \(A_G^\top y\ge \mathbf{1}\), and \(y\le 0\) becomes \(y\ge 0\).

The clean dual is \(\min \mathbf{1}^\top y\) s.t. \(A_G^\top y\ge\mathbf{1},\ y\ge 0\), i.e. per edge \((u,v)\), \(y_u+y_v\ge 1\).
